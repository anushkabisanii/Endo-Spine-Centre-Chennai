Conditions and treatments

Not sure what is causing your pain? Book a consultation for a clear diagnosis.

Lumbar and cervical

Slip disc

A bulging disc pressing on a nerve, causing pain, numbness or weakness.

Lower back and leg

Sciatica

Pain that travels from the lower back through the buttock and down the leg.

Lumbar

Spinal stenosis

A narrowed spinal canal that causes leg pain and weakness when walking.

Cervical

Neck pain

Disc problems in the neck that cause stiffness and arm pain or tingling.

Lumbar

Lower back pain

Persistent back pain that has not improved with rest or medication.

Lumbar

Spondylolisthesis

A vertebra slipping out of position, leading to back and leg pain.

Spine deformity

Spinal deformities

Abnormal curvature of the spine, such as scoliosis, treated with advanced correction techniques.

Procedure

Spinal fusion

Joins unstable vertebrae to stabilise the spine and relieve persistent pain.
